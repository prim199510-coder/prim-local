About Us

Great History Of Our Campus

Mr. PA. Balan, Founder, Chairman and Managing Director of Padmarajam Institute of Management, believes that education can unlock potential and create opportunities for everyone.

Established in 1995, Padmarajam Institute of Management (PRIM) — a professional coaching institute based in Madurai — was founded with the vision of creating quality professionals and has been a stepping stone in the success journey of thousands of aspiring students.

PRIM is celebrated for its eminent faculty members, best-in-class infrastructure, conducive learning environment, and a student-first ecosystem that nurtures both knowledge and confidence.

It is recognized as “one of the leading coaching institutes for professional courses in Accountancy and Management in Tamil Nadu.”

PRIM is also the first institution in India recognized by the International Accreditation & Recognition Council (IAF), Australia, reaffirming its commitment to global academic standards.
